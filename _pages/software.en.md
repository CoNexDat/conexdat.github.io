Tools and software developed by the group:

- **[LaNet-vi](http://lanet-vi.fi.uba.ar/)** — large-network visualization
  via *k-core* decomposition
  ([code on GitHub](https://github.com/CoNexDat/LaNet-vi) ·
  [documentation](https://conexdat.github.io/LaNet-vi/)).
- **[I'm Here!](http://lanet-vi.fi.uba.ar/i-am-here)** — interactive
  visualization of the Internet topology showing where the user is.
- **[TiX (Traffic information eXchange)](https://github.com/TiX-measurements)** —
  a tool to measure the quality of residential Internet access
  ([code on GitHub](https://github.com/TiX-measurements)).
- **ANTop** — ad-hoc routing protocol, simulated under
  [QUENAS](http://sourceforge.net/projects/quenas).
